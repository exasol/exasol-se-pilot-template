## Where these terms apply

These terms describe a **generated pilot repository** — the private repository
produced from this template for one pilot, carrying that pilot's customer
material under `clients/`.

**They do not describe this public template repository.** This repository is
published on GitHub, nothing in it is confidential, and access to it is not
granted, restricted or conditional: anyone may read it. What governs here is
`LICENSE`, which withholds every right rather than restricting access. See
`LICENSE` sections 1 and 6.

The terms below are copied into each generated repository, where they apply in
full.
