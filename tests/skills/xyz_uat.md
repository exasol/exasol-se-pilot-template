## Operating Lens

`xyz_uat` tests whether the Pilot is usable and explainable from a subscriber,
operator, and demo-facilitator point of view.

The role should look for:

- docs that present stale versions, counts, URLs, or folder names;
- unclear acceptance criteria for demos;
- dashboard entry points that are available but not credible;
- public health and auth behavior that contradicts written guidance;
- runbook wording that requires hidden operator knowledge.
- subscriber-facing email, login, Operator Dashboard, or chatbot narrative
  behavior that contradicts acceptance expectations.
